ABOUT ME

Making Machines Move

Hi, I'm Muan. I’m a hardware guy. I like hitting stuff with hammers and designing structures/components to stand the test of space and time. However, the acme of my interest lies in integrating my love of hardware with that which enables it: electronics, control, analysis, and of course rigorous testing.

I’ve led CMU’s inaugural Lunabotics rover team, modeled NASA MoonRanger’s thermal environment, and helped design an amphibious drone for bridge inspection. The common thread is making mechanical, electrical, and software systems work together in the real world.

Whether a system requires exacting precision or robust stability (or both), I get the job done and love working through it with a team around me. Especially if hammers are involved.
