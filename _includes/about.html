ABOUT ME

I work best where bolts, wires, and code all have to agree.

I am a Mechanical Engineering M.S. student at Carnegie Mellon focused on end-to-end mechatronics: requirements, CAD, analysis, fabrication, controls, integration, and test. My recent work includes leading CMU’s lunar excavation rover, modeling MoonRanger’s transit thermal environment, building an automated coil winder for Westinghouse, and comparing motion planners for quadrotors.

I am pursuing full-time roles after Fall 2026 in space or field robotics, hardware integration, and test—especially on teams where the robot eventually has to leave the slide deck and do something useful.
